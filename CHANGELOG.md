# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 18 November 2026

- Hunter pets on the zone maps: a dot in the colour of the beast's family. Point at a dot to see the beast, its levels and its family, and whether it is rare or elite.
- Show one dot per beast, in the middle of where it is found, or a dot for every spawn.
- Hunter pets on the minimap: the beasts near you, in the same colours and with the same choices as on the world map. The dots follow the minimap's zoom, indoors and outdoors, and turn with the minimap when Rotate Minimap is on.
- Show on minimap is a choice of its own. With the world map's dots off, the minimap shows one dot per beast.
- A button in the top right corner of the world map opens a menu with the choices.
- Show all hunter pets, only rares or no rares.
- Pick the families and the beasts in the zone to show.
- The same choices on the Pet Map page in the options.
